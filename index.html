Please remember
this

You do not have to be perfect or bright every day to be cherished. You are already more than enough, exactly as you are — in the quiet moments, the tired moments, and every moment in between.

I hope you give yourself the same patience and warmth you so freely offer everyone else. Rest when you need to. Keep a little softness for yourself.

And when life feels heavy, may you remember there are people cheering for you sincerely, from wherever they are. Your happiness matters, too.
