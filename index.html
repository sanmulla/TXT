page one · the beginning

Dear Soobin,

I hope this finds you in a soft, unhurried moment, with a warm drink nearby and nowhere you need to be for just a little while.

I made this tiny letterbook to leave a little warmth with you. Think of each page as a small pressed flower: a gentle thing to find on an ordinary day.

There is no need to read quickly. Turn the pages whenever you're ready. Every word was written with care, and with a grateful heart.

A little piece

Even from far away, you make someone's ordinary days a little more beautiful. I hope you feel a bit of that love coming back to you today.

page five · a little promise

Please remember
this

You do not have to be perfect or bright every day to be cherished. You are already more than enough, exactly as you are — in the quiet moments, the tired moments, and every moment in between.

I hope you give yourself the same patience and warmth you so freely offer everyone else. Rest when you need to. Keep a little softness for yourself.

And when life feels heavy, may you remember there are people cheering for you sincerely, from wherever they are. Your happiness matters, too.
