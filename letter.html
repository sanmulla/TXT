A letter for you

Take a breath. This one's just for you. ♡

dearest soobin,♡   with love

I hope this finds you in a gentle moment, with a warm drink nearby and nowhere you need to be for just a little while.

You may never know how much happiness your smile brings. It has this lovely way of making a long day feel lighter, like someone opened the curtains and let the morning in.

In a world that can sometimes feel overwhelming, your presence feels like a little piece of sunshine. Your thoughtful words, your careful heart, and the way you look after the people around you remind me that gentleness is its own kind of strength.

Thank you for sharing your voice, your laughter, your hard work, and all those little pieces of yourself with MOA. Thank you for leading with kindness and for letting us grow alongside you. I hope you know that you never have to be perfect to be loved; you are already more than enough exactly as you are.

Even from miles away, you make someone's ordinary days a little more beautiful. I hope that when you need comfort, you can remember there are people cheering for you quietly, sincerely, and always.

Please take your time, rest when you need to, and keep a little softness for yourself. You deserve every bit of warmth you give to everyone else.

Thank you for being you, Soobin.
With endless love,
a little moa ♡
